# Docu4ge

A fast, private PDF toolkit for Windows. Merge, split, compress, edit, annotate, sign, redact, convert and OCR your PDFs. Everything runs on your computer, and your files are never uploaded.

## Download

Get the latest installer from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest) and download `Docu4ge-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). No admin rights needed.

## Installing

1. Run the installer.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC / Unknown publisher"**. This is expected for this early version, because the app is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open Docu4ge from the Start menu, or right-click any PDF and choose **Open with > Docu4ge**.

## Updates

Docu4ge checks for updates automatically. When one is ready you'll see a small notice. Click **Restart** to install it, or it installs the next time you close the app.

## Feedback

Found a problem or have an idea? Open an [issue](../../issues/new) and include:
- what you did and what you expected
- your Windows version
- the Docu4ge version (Help > About)
- a screenshot if it helps

## Privacy

Docu4ge processes files locally on your device. It does not upload your documents.

## Uninstalling

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Docu4ge > Uninstall.
